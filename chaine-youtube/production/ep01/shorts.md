# EP01 — Les 3 Shorts

Chacun autonome, 40–55 s, sous-titres incrustés, même grammaire visuelle (document + cercle rouge). Publication J+1, J+3, J+5 après le long.

## Short 1 — « The Second Wire »

> Visuel : la colonne des 11 virements, défilement rapide, arrêt net sur le n°2, cercle rouge.

A bank CEO sent eleven wire transfers to scammers. Forty-seven million dollars. But investigators will tell you the story isn't the first wire. [PAUSE] The first wire is a catastrophic mistake — a man in crisis making one terrible call. [PAUSE] The second wire is different. The second wire means he looked at the hole he'd made — and instead of reporting it, he covered it. [PAUSE] Nine more followed. The bank died on the eleventh. [PAUSE] In these cases, the point of no return is almost never the first payment. It's the one that hides it.

## Short 2 — « The College Fund »

> Visuel : trois lignes de relevé reconstituées — 40 000 $ / 10 000 $ / 60 000 $ — surlignées une à une.

Before this man stole forty-seven million from his own bank, he took forty thousand dollars from his church. [PAUSE] Ten thousand from his friends' investment club. [PAUSE] And sixty thousand dollars from his daughter's college fund. [PAUSE] Here's what that last one tells you. Nobody spends their kid's tuition because they're greedy. He spent it because he was certain the money was coming back — and the scam had spent months building exactly that certainty. [PAUSE] The fake dashboard showed his balance growing the entire time. The money was already gone.

## Short 3 — « Five Banks Failed That Year »

> Visuel : liste FDIC 2023, cinq lignes ; la ligne Heartland se surligne ; communiqué DOJ en fin.

Five American banks failed in two thousand twenty-three. Four of them made national news — interest rates, bank runs, billions in losses. [PAUSE] The fifth was a tiny bank in Elkhart, Kansas. Population: under two thousand. [PAUSE] It didn't fail because of the economy. It failed because its own CEO wired forty-seven million dollars — eleven transfers — to a crypto scam. [PAUSE] The town's investors lost nine million. Their shares went to zero. [PAUSE] He got twenty-four years. The scammers were never identified.
